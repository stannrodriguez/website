About

I'm a full-stack engineer. To me, full stack starts with the user: what they need, how that meets what the business needs, and which features are worth building. Then I sequence it, build it, test it, and own it.

Most recently I built the chat agent at Assembled. Earlier I built workspace and collaboration features at Notion. At Amazon I led the frontend redesign of Prime Gaming and built internal developer tools. Before that I worked on social features at Sonder Social. I studied statistics and actuarial math at UCLA and started in finance as a risk and quantitative analyst at Capital Group.

Outside work, I make small sites and tools about whatever I'm curious about. They're all on the projects page.
